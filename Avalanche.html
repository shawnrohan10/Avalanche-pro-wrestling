Description of Avalanche

Avalanche is a pro-wrestling company. We are our own pro-wrestling company indepent of other promotions. We have our regular scheduled shows on Netflix at 8:00 p.m. to 10:00 p.m every Friday. We are a small start up company at the moment, but we want to grow and are actively trying to. This is modern pro-wrestling, and it is done right by Avalanche.

About us

Avalanche was founded by 2 friends who loved wrestling as children and as adults. As fans we knew what story lines we liked and what we wanted to see. Now that we have our own pro-wrestling company, we put our creative minds behind it to give a show that we would have liked to see growing up, or a show we would have liked to see now. We will take the modern pro-wrestling to new heights if possible.
